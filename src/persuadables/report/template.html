persuadables

Who does a marketing campaign actually persuade? The experiment readout, uplift models that find the customers worth emailing, and what targeting them is worth.

1. Did the emails work?

Average treatment effects against the no-email holdout. z-test for the rates, Welch's t-test plus a bootstrap interval for spend, CUPED with pre-period covariates, and Holm correction across both emails and all three metrics.

Rates in percentage points, spend in dollars per customer. MDE: minimum detectable effect at 80% power, alpha 0.05, for the realised arm sizes. CUPED VR: share of variance removed by the pre-period covariates.

2. Who responds?

Effects within pre-registered segments, fixed in SQL before any effect was estimated. The p-value is Cochran's Q test that the effect is the same in every level, Holm-adjusted across all segment tests.

3. Can a model find the persuadables?

Qini coefficient: area between the Qini curve and random targeting, normalized so 0 = random and 1 = oracle (95% bootstrap CI over test customers). Bold: the learner chosen on the training split.

4. Policy simulator

Email the top k% of test customers by predicted uplift, each with the email the model expects to work best on them, and compare with a response model, random targeting and emailing everyone. Values are off-policy estimates (self-normalized inverse propensity weighting) on the held-out randomized test split; each tile shows that policy's own 95% bootstrap interval.
